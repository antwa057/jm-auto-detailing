Our Story

Built on Pride, Precision & Local Trust

J&M Auto Care & Detailing is a locally owned detailing studio based in Moncton, New Brunswick.

We started J&M because too many drivers in Moncton were choosing between rushed automatic car washes and detailers who overpromise and underdeliver. We wanted to build something different: a shop that treats every sedan, SUV, and truck that comes through our doors like it's our own — with real attention to detail, honest pricing, and results you can see and feel.

Whether you're prepping a vehicle for resale, need a deep interior clean after a long winter, or just want your daily driver to feel brand new again, our team takes the time to do it right. We use professional-grade products and proven techniques for every wash and detail — no shortcuts.
